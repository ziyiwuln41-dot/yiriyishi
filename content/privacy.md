## Who runs this
一日一诗 is a free, non-commercial Chinese poetry and 成语 newsletter run by Ziyi in Singapore. Questions about your data: **[contact email]**. I am the person responsible for data protection for this site.

## What I collect
- **Your email address.** That is the only thing you give me.
- **When you signed up and confirmed**, and **which emails have been sent to you**, so you don't receive duplicates.

I do **not** collect your name, age, school, location, or phone number. Emails contain no open-tracking or click-tracking.

## Why I collect it
Only to send you the daily email (6:00am SGT) and the weekly Monday introduction, and to let you unsubscribe. I will never use your email for advertising, and I will never sell, rent, or share it with anyone.

## Consent
You sign up yourself, then confirm by clicking a link in a confirmation email. Nothing is sent until you confirm. If you are under 13, please ask a parent or guardian before signing up.

## Unsubscribing and deletion
Every email has an **unsubscribe link** at the bottom. One click stops all emails, and your address is **deleted within 30 days**. You can also email **[contact email]** to:
- ask what data I hold about you,
- correct your email address, or
- ask me to delete it.

I'll reply within 30 days.

## Where your data is stored
- **Database:** Supabase, hosted in the Singapore region.
- **Email delivery:** Resend, which may process your email address on servers outside Singapore (including the United States) in order to deliver messages. Resend is bound by its data processing agreement to protect it.
- **Website hosting:** Vercel.

These providers only handle your email address to run the service. They may not use it for anything else.

## Keeping it safe
Your email is stored in a database that the public cannot read. Access is limited to me, protected with strong passwords and two-factor authentication. If a data breach ever happens that could harm you, I will tell you and, where required, the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC), as soon as practicable.

## How long I keep it
Only while you are subscribed. Unconfirmed sign-ups are deleted after 7 days.

## Content
Classical poems are in the public domain. Explanations and commentary are written or edited by me; AI tools may help draft some explanations, and everything is reviewed before sending. If you believe any content infringes your rights, contact **[contact email]** and I will remove it promptly.

## Changes
If this notice changes in a way that matters, I'll tell subscribers by email before the change takes effect.
